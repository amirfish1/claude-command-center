How this is counted

Where do these numbers come from?

Agent work value totals every token your sessions used and prices it at public API list rates, including cached tokens. It answers: "what would this workload have cost without a subscription?"

You paid spreads your plan's monthly price across this period by calendar day, so "today" shows roughly one day's slice of your plan.

Return on plan is value divided by plan cost. At 1x you break even; higher means the subscription is paying for itself.

Free runs saved counts sessions that ran on the free model router at $0, priced at Sonnet rates. If the router reports more traffic than CCC launched, the difference counts too.

Tokens priced at fallback rates because the model name is unrecognised are included and marked as estimates. Nothing here is sent anywhere; it is read from local transcripts on this machine.
