## What changed

- `ccc_leadgen/segments.py` (new): classifies a lead into `heavy_user`,
  `multi_engine`, or `token_limits` from repo description/topics/readme
  already on the Lead record, no extra API calls.
- `ccc_leadgen/sequence.py` (new): 3-email sequence x 3 segments (9 templates
  total), short/benefit-first/plain-text/one-CTA/no-hype/no-em-dash. Copy now
  covers orchestration (spawn/steer/ask/group chats), WatchTower queues
  closing on a commit SHA, the eight-engine board, and the `ccc models`
  cost/effort catalog — replacing the old single generic template that
  predated most of those features.
- `ccc_leadgen/draft.py`: `render_email()` (used by `./ccc-leadgen draft`)
  now delegates to the new `render_sequence_email(lead, step=1)`, so the
  existing CLI pipeline is unchanged in shape, just segment-aware. Steps 2/3
  exist as functions but nothing schedules them yet (no follow-up cron —
  future work, out of scope here).
- `scripts/render_outbox_preview.py` (new): dry-run renders step-1 emails
  for up to N sample qualified leads (spread across segments) to
  `output/outbox-preview/*.eml`. Never sends, never touches
  `state/leads.jsonl`.
- `scripts/build_review_html.py` (new): builds
  `output/outbox-preview-review.html`, a one-page review of all 9 templates
  plus the rendered samples, for Amir to approve before the first real send.
  `output/` is gitignored (real prospect emails), so this file is local-only
  by design — open it directly on disk.
- `tests/test_sequence.py` (new): 6 tests for segment classification and
  sequence rendering (em-dash check, subject placeholders, step count).
  Full suite: 50 passed.
