`GET /api/telemetry/health-report`: a local, nothing-sent 24-hour health snapshot (view-switch, conversation-open and rail latency histograms with p50/p75/p95, bucketed session, Decision, message, and WatchTower counts) to paste into bug reports. Rail clicks now record `view_switch` timings and the rail's app fetch records `rail_apps`. See docs/telemetry.md.
