The q2 queue board now carries a **Status brief** band between the flow diagram and the ticket list: a content analysis of the selected queue's open/needs-input tickets that leads with commonalities and shared root causes across tickets (e.g. "8 of 14 tickets are one Arketa credit-ledger bug"), then the decisions the owner must make, then next steps. Generated by a one-shot `claude -p` (sonnet-5) call on a daemon thread, cached per queue under the CCC state dir with per-ticket signatures, so the band can say "Last updated 12m ago · 3 ticket updates since" and only auto-regenerates on first visit or when ≥3 tickets changed — everything else is the manual ↻. Ticket refs inside the brief are clickable and select the ticket; the band collapses per queue and respects both themes.
