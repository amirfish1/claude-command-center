- The auto-pick **Why?** panel now has two tabs. **Allowances** shows each account's pace on a bar centred on "on pace" (ahead = quota that will expire unused, behind = running short), with banked resets and plan end dates as tokens and the account's models as one-click chips. **Ranking** lets you drag to reorder the Deep and Routine lists and drag a "good enough" line.
- Auto-pick is now expiry-first: among models above your good-enough line, the allowance that would waste the most per hour wins (a daily window expiring tonight beats weekly spare). Behind-pace accounts sink unless a banked reset can refill them, and a plan end date counts everything left as expiring then.
